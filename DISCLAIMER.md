# Disclaimer

SeedForge v0.3.0-alpha is a **research prototype design**. Read this before building or using it.

## What SeedForge is not

* not an industrial production line;
* not a certified or safety-rated machine (the wiring is research-prototype wiring);
* not a chemically validated wood-processing system (it does not include or automate chemical
  treatment of veneer);
* not a biologically validated seed product (nothing here shows that carriers made with it
  germinate, bury themselves or survive in the field);
* not a flight-certified payload or an aircraft design.

## Current evidence

As of this release the machine has **not been physically built**. CAD has been checked for
geometry and interference, firmware compiles and passes host unit tests, and the host software,
simulator and vision pipeline pass tests on synthetic data. Those are not physical results.
Renders and animations are CAD visualizations, not photographs; the self-burying animation is a
conceptual visualization, not a validated physics simulation.

## Risks you accept

Building and operating the machine involves rotating shafts, pinch points, cutters, hot stepper
drivers, mains-powered supplies and possibly batteries. Seeds and plant material can be invasive or
regulated. Aerial deployment is regulated and may require permits. You are responsible for your own
safety, for supervision of students, for institutional approvals and for complying with local law.
See [docs/safety.md](docs/safety.md).
