# SeedForge R3 source basis

Primary research reference:

- Danli Luo et al., "Autonomous self-burying seed carriers for aerial seeding," Nature 614, 463-470 (2023). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-022-05656-3.

Useful accessible mirrors/preprint used for dimensional cross-checks:

- NSF Public Access Repository mirror of the Nature article: https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10432209
- ResearchSquare/preprint material containing supplementary modelling figures: https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-1341317/v1_covered.pdf

Source-grounded points used in R3:

- three-tailed carrier architecture;
- seven-coil examples;
- 0.5 mm veneer thickness and 0.8 mm mandrel examples;
- favourable approximately 25-30 degree resting angle for the three-tail architecture;
- whitebark-pine payload example around 11 mm long;
- supplementary simulation examples with 2.5 mm tail/body width and tail reference lengths 27, 29.5, and 32.5 mm;
- published actuator examples around 3 mm width on a 0.8 mm mandrel.

The detailed dry-tail spline geometry and tip surface in the delivered CAD are engineering approximations for fixture and clearance design. They are not the authors' original CAD.
